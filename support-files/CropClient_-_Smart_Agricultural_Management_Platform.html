About CropClient

Built on UC Research

CropClient is powered by decades of University of California research in soil science, irrigation, and crop management. Our platform integrates field-tested methodologies with real-time data to provide scientifically-backed recommendations.

Regulatory Compliance

Navigate California's complex agricultural regulations with confidence. CropClient helps you meet SGMA (Sustainable Groundwater Management Act) requirements and Irrigated Lands Management Program standards, avoiding costly penalties.

Proven Results

Field trials demonstrate that CropClient reduces water and fertilizer use by 20-40% while maintaining crop yields. Growers like Mark Mason in Salinas have successfully reduced nitrogen usage by 30% using our platform.

Expert Leadership

Led by UC Cooperative Extension experts like Michael Cahn (Ph.D. in soil science), our team combines academic research with practical field experience to deliver reliable agricultural solutions.

Free to Use

Supported by grants from the California Department of Food and Agriculture, Department of Water Resources, and USDA. CropClient is completely free to use, with donations supporting ongoing improvements.

Proven Track Record

Since 2011, CropClient has provided over 72,000 recommendations to growers across California, helping optimize agricultural operations with science-based tools.

Platform Features

🤖

AI-Powered Analytics

Advanced machine learning algorithms analyze crop data, weather patterns, and soil conditions to provide intelligent irrigation and fertilization recommendations.

👥

Role-Based Access

Secure multi-level access control with Admin, Manager, and Field roles, ensuring the right people have access to the right data and controls.

🔍

Advanced Data Filtering

Sophisticated filtering and analytics dashboard allows users to drill down into specific crops, time periods, fields, and performance metrics.

📡

Field Integration

Real-time sensor integration and field monitoring with automated data collection from irrigation systems, weather stations, and soil sensors.

📊

Compliance Reporting

Automated generation of detailed reports for SGMA and regulatory requirements, with customizable templates and scheduling options.

🌱

Multi-Crop Modeling

Predictive modeling for 20+ crop types with growth stage tracking, yield forecasting, and resource optimization recommendations.
